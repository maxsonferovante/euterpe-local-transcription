# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful and welcoming to everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept feedback and differing viewpoints in good faith.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted advances.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or doxxing.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Disruptive conduct in project spaces or sustained bad-faith engagement.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy to the project maintainers through a private channel listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent practical. Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or remove a participant from project spaces. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and documented for consistency.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
